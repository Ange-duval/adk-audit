Run structured, professional audits from a single app

ADK-AUDIT structures internal and external audits — business and QHSE — from checklist to closure. Auditors record findings with photo evidence, track corrective actions, and management gets a live compliance dashboard.

Key features

📊

Compliance dashboard

Live KPIs: compliance rate, findings by severity, open vs resolved anomalies, and deadline tracking.

✅

Business & QHSE audits

Two audit types out of the box, each with its own reference checklist and workflow: Draft → In Progress → Closed.

📋

Reusable checklists

Build a catalogue of points to audit per audit type and service, and load it in one click on any new audit.

📷

Findings with photo evidence

Every non-conformity becomes a findings record: severity, description, photos, and status (Open → In Progress → Resolved).

🛠️

Corrective action plans

Link each finding to an action plan with an owner and due date, and track overdue items at a glance.

📄

Professional PDF reports

Generate a clean audit report directly from the audit record, ready to share with stakeholders.

How it works

A simple 5-step process, from setup to compliance monitoring

1

Set up your reference data

Declare your locations/sites (with the responsible person and assigned staff), your services, and build your catalogue of points to audit per audit type. This becomes the reusable foundation for every future audit.

2

Plan the audit and load the checklist

Create an audit, choose its type (Business or QHSE), assign auditors and dates, then load the standard checklist in one click. Go through each point and mark it compliant or non-compliant.

3

Document findings with photo evidence

Every non-conforming point automatically generates a finding. Record the severity, a clear description, and attach photos as proof — everything an auditor needs to justify the non-conformity.

4

Track corrective actions to closure

Each finding is linked to an action plan with a responsible person and a due date. Overdue items are flagged automatically, so nothing falls through the cracks.

5

Close the audit and monitor compliance

Once all findings are resolved, close the audit — the compliance rate updates automatically. The dashboard then gives management a live view of compliance, findings by severity, and deadlines across every audit.
